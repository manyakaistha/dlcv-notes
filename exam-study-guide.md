# Exam Study Guide

[[Knowledge-Map|Knowledge Map]] · [[exam-question-index|Question index]]

Choose the route for your exam. Work P1 first, then P2, then P3; study prerequisite concepts in order and practise the linked questions without looking at their answers.

Each occurrence is one manifest question assigned to its primary topic. Score = occurrences × mean marks = sum of assigned marks; repeated questions count separately. Mean is undefined for zero occurrences. Rank is competition rank across the applicable route, with tied scores sharing rank. For each route, P1 has a positive score at least 50% of that route’s maximum; P2 has a positive score at least 20% but below 50%; P3 is below 20%, including zero. Cut-offs describe this evidence, not a prediction or the importance of prerequisites.

Weeks 6–12 are **provisional**: topics inferred from past papers; replace when official syllabus is available. Topic-bank links lead to the topic heading when present, otherwise to the week bank. In-progress banks may not yet contain every question.
