# Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem

The Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem states that a continuous bandlimited signal containing maximum frequency component $f_{\max}$ can be perfectly reconstructed from its discrete samples if and only if the sampling frequency $f_s$ satisfies:
$$f_s \ge 2 f_{\max}$$

The critical boundary $f_N = rac{f_s}{2}$ is defined as the **Nyquist frequency**.
