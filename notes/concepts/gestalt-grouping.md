# Gestalt Grouping Principles in Computer Vision

Originating in early 20th-century psychology (Wertheimer, Koffka, Kohler), Gestalt theory asserts that visual perception groups local elements into holistic perceptual wholes (*"The whole is greater than the sum of its parts"*).

### Core Grouping Laws
1. **Proximity:** Elements close to each other are grouped together.
2. **Similarity:** Elements sharing color, texture, or shape are grouped together.
3. **Continuity (Good Continuation):** Smooth, continuous curves are preferred over sharp directional changes.
4. **Closure:** Incomplete boundaries are mentally completed to form closed figures.
5. **Symmetry:** Symmetrical regions are preferentially perceived as coherent objects.
6. **Common Fate:** Elements moving with identical velocity vectors are grouped into single entities.
